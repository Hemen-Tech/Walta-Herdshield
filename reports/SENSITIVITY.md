# Sensitivity of the decision to the assumptions

Tested 5 markets x 3 animal conditions x 4 drought scenarios, nudging one assumption at a time.

| Assumption nudged (-50% / +50%; drift -1% / +1% per month) | Share of tests where the advice changed |
|---|---|
| mortality | 8% |
| thinning | 4% |
| holding_cost | 4% |
| crash_effect | 25% |
| price_drift | 12% |

| Drought scenario | Share of tests where the advice changed |
|---|---|
| No Drought | 7% |
| Mild | 3% |
| Moderate | 7% |
| Severe | 27% |

When advice changed it moved by one step in 65 cases and by two or more steps in 0 cases.

Reading: where the share is high, that assumption MUST be replaced with local evidence before the advice is trusted.
